District by Zomato

“Zomato for everything you do outside your home.”

Just as Zomato powers food delivery and Blinkit leads quick commerce, District is Zomato’s third standalone powerhouse. It consolidates fragmented offline lifestyle bookings — dining out, live concerts, movies, sports, and nightlife — into one unified real-world experiences platform.

✨ The Signature Feature · Social Commerce Layer

ddle by district

“Stop the 80-text group chat debate. Booked in 30 seconds.”

Doodle is an AI social canvas built directly into District. Instead of endless WhatsApp back-and-forth, the organiser prompts natural intent. Doodle holds seats across multi-stop venues, creates a 30-sec WhatsApp mini-app, collects anonymous votes & diets, and auto-splits bills via 1-tap UPI.

⚡ 30-Sec Web Mini-App (No App Install) 🛡️ Anonymous Preference Shield 💸 1-Tap SplitPay via UPI 🪄 Self-Healing Dynamic Timeline
🎯 The Problem Space · Why It Is Needed

The "Group Chat Paradox": Why 62% of Outings Drop Off

Outings don't die from lack of enthusiasm — they die from coordination fatigue, social anxiety, and payment friction.

🗣️ Coordination Chaos

The 84-Text Group Chat Black Hole

The Pain: “Where should we go?”, “Anything is fine!”, “Not Italian again pls”. Endless banter with zero decisions. Hours drag on, cinema seats vanish, dinner tables are lost, and the outing is abandoned.

Doodle Fix: 1 Interactive Canvas Link. Replaces 84 texts with a 30-second decision card dropped directly into WhatsApp.

🤫 Social Anxiety

Dietary & Budget Peer Pressure

The Pain: Friends hesitate to speak up about tight budgets or niche diets (vegan, Jain, halal) to avoid feeling high-maintenance or awkward in front of the squad.

Doodle Fix: The Anonymous Shield. Attendance is confirmed openly, but budget sliders and venue A/B votes remain strictly anonymous.

💸 Payment Stress

The Organiser’s Burden & Money Chasing

The Pain: One well-meaning friend fronts ₹6,000+ on their card and spends the next 3 weeks chasing people on GPay/UPI or computing complicated split sheets.

Doodle Fix: Instant 1-Tap SplitPay via UPI. District dispatches itemised UPI collection links directly. Vendors are settled instantly.

⚙️ Product Architecture · The 5 Core Pillars

How Doodle Orchestrates Seamless Squad Experiences

Designed specifically around Indian urban nightlife, food culture, and UPI payment dynamics.

1
Natural Intent to Canvas

Speak naturally (e.g. “Birthday in T Nagar, 6 friends, one vegan”). AI secures real-time table & ticket holds with zero advance fee.

2
Zero-Install WhatsApp Mini-App

Friends tap a WhatsApp link and vote in 30 seconds inside mobile Safari/Chrome. Zero app download or account creation required.

3
Anonymous Consensus Radar

Blind A/B venue voting and private spend sliders ensure honest squad preferences without peer pressure or debates.

4
Direct UPI SplitPay

Everyone pays their equal share via UPI / GPay deep links. The organiser's screen updates live from Pending to Paid ✓.

5
Dynamic Auto-Heal Engine

When a movie show shifts by 45 mins, Doodle recalibrates subsequent dinner holds automatically with 1-tap host approval.
