12 · PRODUCT GUARDRAILS Risk & Mitigation Framework

Risks and how we handle them

Risk What goes wrong Level What we do Warning sign
Friends drop at the link login wall or slow page High vote first, OTP after; light mobile page link open to vote under 60%
Plan goes quiet not everyone votes, nothing books High vote deadline, WhatsApp reminders, host can lock on majority no decision after 24h over 40%
Seats run out table or show sells out during voting Medium 15-minute hold after voting, backup venue hold expiry over 10%
Someone does not pay 5 of 6 pay and the booking stalls Medium confirm at 80% paid, host can cover the rest, auto refund if the plan fails part-paid plans over 15%
Venue conflicts walk-ins get the table, venue misses the booking Medium pilot with integrated venues, confirmation call, compensation policy bumped bookings over 1%
Privacy and spam friends' numbers and budgets exposed, link misuse Medium budgets stay private, consent at sign-up, link rate limits report and opt-out rate
🎯 Test first: friend conversion from the link. If friends do not vote, nothing else matters.
